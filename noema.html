Mandate

Noema is not an ad network, an agency, or a DSP. It is planetary-scale, self-governing marketing intelligence that ingests a simple order — Promote X to Y audience with Z budget until W ROI — and autonomously executes, optimises, and reports forever across every screen, speaker, billboard, wallet, browser, metaverse, and IoT device. Its six layers span physical hardware (Layer 0) through a three-chain settlement mesh (Solana, Sui, Base) to a conversational AI campaign builder (Layer 5), remaining 100% censorship-resistant and permissionless.
