I build verification systems — software designed to check its own work. My practice is turning documented logic into production-grade systems, with explicit scope and performance that can be inspected.

My first deployed system is CiteProof: deterministic citation verification for legal briefs, with a published accuracy ledger. It sets the standard for what I build next.

The question driving my work is the gap between what systems process and what they genuinely understand. I approach it through concrete verification problems, making each system’s claims testable and its limits visible.

I work independently from Nairobi. No company or institutional affiliation — just documented work, judged by what ships.
