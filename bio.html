Biography

My passion is solving hard problems — by turning ideas into architectures, into systems that define the edge of what can be done.

That is my life's work.

I work independently from Nairobi, Kenya, as a builder. My practice is simple: take documented architectural logic and translate it into production-grade, sovereign deep-tech systems — without dilution, without feature creep, without compromise.

The world's defining crisis is Interpretive Intelligence: systems that process everything but understand nothing. I build to close that gap. My first deployed system is CiteProof: deterministic citation verification for legal briefs — every finding traceable to evidence, every limit stated. It is the standard everything I build next has to meet.

I keep my work under a personal discipline I wrote for myself — because great visions die a thousand small deaths from dilution, and I refuse to let that happen. Designs are documented before they are built, and nothing ships that I cannot defend line by line.

I anchored this in Kenya out of conviction: technological sovereignty is data on sovereign soil, elite talent cultivated here, intellectual property forged here and deployed globally.

I am a builder. My work is not a startup — it is long-horizon independent work, judged by what ships. And I am only getting started.
