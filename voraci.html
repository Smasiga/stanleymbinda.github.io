Project Voraci

Volumetric Omnidomain Recognition, Anticipation & Cognitive Intelligence

An anticipatory intelligence architecture that synthesises legally accessible data into permanent, compounding foresight.

Mandate

Every intelligence agency is built to protect secrets. Voraci is built to understand everything that is not one — and that is more than enough to see the future. Its six-layer stack runs from a Collection Layer through Multi-Modal Fusion and a custom Hypergraph Engine, into an Anticipatory Engine using causal inference, a Decision Intelligence Layer with LLM integration, and a Continuous Learning Layer. Version 2.0 closes every prior specification gap: entity resolution, hypergraph storage, alert-volume management at 1B+ entity scale, and single-point-of-failure analysis.
