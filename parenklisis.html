Project Parenklisis

aka Clinamen · The Swerve · 98 / 2 Principle

A human-machine intelligence platform engineered to forecast the direction of large-scale economic, financial, and geopolitical change with ~98% qualitative confidence — while accepting the 2% swerve it cannot time.

Mandate

Parenklisis operates on a foundational asymmetry: the structural trajectory of civilisation-scale systems is governed by two invariant laws — Entropy and the Human Efficiency Mandate — whose combined operation renders directional outcomes near-certain, even as precise timing stays obscured by irreducible agency, reflexivity, and contingency. The platform ingests real-time global data across ten vector categories, maps structural stress through a causal knowledge graph, and surfaces repeatable historical patterns via similarity matching. It does not eliminate the swerve. It is architected to survive it.
