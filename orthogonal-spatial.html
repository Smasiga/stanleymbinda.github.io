Orthogonal Spatial

aka The Interpretive Renderer · 3D Topology · Ghost Discovery

A spatial intelligence layer that resolves software dependency graphs in real time and renders them as navigable 3D cities — towers are modules, conduits are data flows, colour is health.

Mandate

Software engineering is the last major profession without a spatial visualisation layer. Architects have CAD. Surgeons have MRI. Engineers have tree and grep. Orthogonal Spatial closes that gap. It ingests any repository, resolves its dependency graph in real time, and renders it as an interactive 3D city — a 20-million-line monorepo becomes a skyline you can navigate in seconds. Beyond structure, it binds live telemetry to the model: every node carries health, every conduit carries flow. Unregistered services are discovered as ghost nodes and promoted into the topology when their presence stabilises. Failures are classified by a dual-path AI engine — primary inference, fallback inference, then threshold mathematics — so the system does not merely show what changed; it explains why.
