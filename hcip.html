HCIP

The Skeleton · The Nervous System · The Soul

A holistic code intelligence platform that understands software as structure, behaviour, and intent — a partner, not a tool.

Mandate

HCIP treats code as a living artifact across three layers: the Skeleton (structure), the Nervous System (behaviour and data flow), and the Soul (intent). The IDE plugin is the primary surface — where hover panels explain intent, proactive warnings prevent mistakes, and recommendations appear at the moment they are needed. The plugin is local-first, sub-300ms, offline-capable, and capped at 200 MB with under 1% idle CPU. The IR schema is the authoritative data model binding all three layers through a Universal Entity Identifier.
