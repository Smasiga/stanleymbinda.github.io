Mandate

The Veritatem Substratum is the structural response to the hallucination economy. Its Three Oaths — that data carry its origin, reasoning expose its trace, and authority operate within its bounds — are not design options but the minimum viable architecture for any system granted the power to alter physical reality. Backed by the Charter of the Commons and mechanised formal verification (Tamarin Prover + Coq), the V-Substrate makes every assertion auditable, every reasoning trace transparent, and every intervention bound to explicit sovereign human authority.
