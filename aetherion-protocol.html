The Aetherion Protocol

Six-Layer Stack · Four-Dimensional Utility · Economically Irrational to Corrupt

A fully decentralised, immutable protocol that distinguishes legitimate tokens from fraudulent ones at scale, in real time, without any central authority.

Mandate

The Aetherion is a new kind of institution: a protocol governed by mathematical laws encoded at genesis. It assesses every token across a four-dimensional utility framework, monitors every trade across every major exchange, renders verdicts through economically incentivised random committees, and enforces those verdicts through a layered stack at the protocol, application, and economic level. It is designed to be self-sustaining, self-correcting, and incorruptible — not because it is run by trustworthy people, but because its architecture makes corruption economically irrational. It is built to outlast everyone alive today.
